📋 Build Profiles
Search modes explained

All three answer questions about your people. The difference is how much information they look at before answering — which affects speed, depth, and cost.

🏃 Quick · ~10k tokens · cheapest, fastest

Skims a one-line summary of every household — name, family members, AGC score, home value, and total giving. It does not read your notes or interactions. Best for fast roster questions like "who are my top-AGC families in Pepper Pike?" Answers in a single paragraph.

⚡ Standard · ~35k tokens · the everyday default

Everything Quick sees, plus it searches your notes and interactions for the ones most relevant to your question and reads those too. Best for most real questions — "who mentioned wanting to host a Shabbat dinner?" — where the answer lives in your notes. Needs embeddings generated to find notes.

🔍 Power Search · ~80–100k tokens · slow & costly (~$0.30)

The deepest look. Reads the full detailed profile of every household — AGC breakdown, gift history, AI research summary, suggested approach, business info — plus the relevant notes. Use for big cross-database thinking like "build me a major-gift prospect list with talking points for each." Asks you to confirm before running because of the cost.

"Tokens" is roughly how much text the AI reads per question. More tokens = more thorough but slower and more expensive. When unsure, start with Standard.
